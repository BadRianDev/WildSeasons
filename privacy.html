Privacy policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how the Wild Seasons app for LG webOS Smart TVs (the “App”), developed by BadRianDev (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: the App does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal data.

1. Information the App collects

None. The App does not ask for your name, email address, account, location, voice, camera, contacts or any other personal information. It does not identify you or your TV.

2. Information stored on your TV

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of game data in your TV's local app storage:

DataPurpose
Best total scoreShown on the title screen
Highest unlocked seasonLets you continue from the season you reached

This data never leaves your TV and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your TV settings.

3. Network, advertising and analytics

The App works completely offline. It makes no network requests and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, cookies, third-party SDKs or in-app purchases.

4. LG and the platform

The App is distributed through the LG Content Store and runs on LG's webOS platform. LG may collect information under its own privacy policy and your TV's settings. We do not receive that information. For details, see LG's privacy policy on your TV or on LG's website.

5. Support requests on GitHub

Support is provided through public contact forms (issues) on GitHub at github.com/BadRianDev/WildSeasons. When you submit a form, your GitHub username and everything you write are publicly visible. That information is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We use it only to answer your request and improve the App. Please don't post personal information. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time, or ask us to remove an issue.

6. Children

The App is a cartoon game suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. Children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) should not use GitHub to contact us without a parent or guardian.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. The App holds no personal data about you. For anything you've posted on GitHub, you can manage it yourself or contact us as described below.

8. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

For general questions and support, please use the support forms.

For privacy matters that can't be raised publicly, email the developer at badriandev@gmail.com. This address is for privacy requests only. Support emails will be redirected to the GitHub forms.
