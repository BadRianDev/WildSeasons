About the game

Wild Seasons is a slapstick shooting-gallery game for LG Smart TVs (webOS). Aim with the Magic Remote or the arrow keys. Bonk bears, wolves, boars, foxes, rabbits, ducks, pheasants and stags across a wide parallax valley. Hits are harmless cartoon fun: swirly eyes, dizzy stars and feathers everywhere.

  • Four seasonal levels, each with a 3-minute timer and a score goal to unlock the next.
  • Animals at three distances. Far shots score up to three times more.
  • Hidden surprises: critters peeking out from behind trees and boulders, and a fish leaping from the summer pond.
  • Watch out for the camouflaged hunters. Shooting one costs you 500 points!
