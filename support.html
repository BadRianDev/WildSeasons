Contact & support

All support goes through our GitHub contact forms. Choose the one that fits, fill it in and submit it. We'll reply on the same page. You need a free GitHub account to submit a form.

🐞 Report a bug Something isn't working, the game freezes, or the graphics look wrong. 💡 Suggest a feature Ideas for new animals, seasons, surprises or game modes. ❓ Ask a question Help with installing, controls, scoring or anything else.

Please note: submitted forms are public. Don't include personal information such as your email address, phone number, home address or TV serial number.

Before you ask

  • Look through the existing requests. Your question may already be answered.
  • Controls: point the Magic Remote or use the arrow keys to aim, press OK to shoot, press any colour key or 0 to reload, and press BACK to pause.
  • Can't reach the goal? Every miss costs 100 points. Take careful shots, aim for far animals (×3) and look out for the hidden bonus critters.

Privacy requests

Privacy or data requests that shouldn't be posted publicly are handled by email. The contact details are in the privacy policy. Please don't use email for general support. We answer support requests only through the forms above.
